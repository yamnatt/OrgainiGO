# 🌱 OrganiGo
### Taste the Organic Revolution

OrganiGo is an agritech startup concept focused on transforming the organic farming ecosystem by directly connecting farmers and consumers while leveraging weather intelligence, IoT technology, certification support, and sustainable supply chains.

The platform aims to eliminate unnecessary middlemen, improve farmer profitability, provide consumers with authentic organic products, and accelerate the transition from conventional farming to organic farming.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Organic farming in India faces several critical challenges:

### Farmers
- Low profits due to multiple middlemen
- Difficulty finding reliable buyers
- Expensive organic certification
- Lack of weather-based decision support
- Limited access to quality biofertilizers and manure
- Pest attacks and reduced yields during conversion

### Consumers
- Difficulty verifying authenticity of organic products
- High product prices
- Limited availability of certified organic produce
- Lack of awareness regarding organic food benefits
- Slow delivery and poor customer experience

## 🔬 Key Innovations

### 1. IoT-Driven Precision Farming
In partnership with agricultural IoT providers, farmers receive:

- Real-time soil monitoring
- Temperature tracking
- Humidity analysis
- Rainfall forecasting
- Irrigation recommendations
- Crop health insights

### 2. Weather Intelligence System

Using:
- Agro Monitoring API
- Historical weather datasets
- LSTM-based prediction models

The platform provides:
- 7-day weather forecasts
- Sowing suitability analysis
- Harvest timing recommendations
- Biofertilizer application alerts
- Irrigation scheduling

---

### 3. Organic Certification Support

OrganiGo simplifies:
- Certification procedures
- Documentation
- Verification processes

This reduces barriers for farmers shifting from conventional to organic farming.

---

### 4. Sustainable Fertilizer Ecosystem

Collaboration Model:

1. Unsold produce → Pig farms
2. Pig farms → Manure supply
3. Manure → Organic farmers
4. Farmers → Organic produce

This creates a circular agricultural economy with minimal waste.
